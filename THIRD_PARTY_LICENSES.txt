1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ZERO-COPYLEFT ISOLATION GUARANTEE
================================================================================

llm-note is a local-first note and notebook engine engineered with an uncompromising
zero external runtime dependencies architecture. It operates exclusively on local SQLite
databases and plain-text files.

- 100% Permissive Dependencies: Core execution relies solely on the Python Standard
  Library (PSF-2.0) and SQLite.
- Zero-Copyleft Isolation Guarantee: No GPL, AGPL, SSPL, or copyleft-encumbered
  libraries are bundled, linked, or distributed at runtime.
- Unprivileged User-Mode (RunAsInvoker): llm-note executes exclusively in standard,
  non-elevated user space without requesting administrative or root privileges.
- Zero Egress: Strictly zero outbound network connections, zero analytics, and zero telemetry.
